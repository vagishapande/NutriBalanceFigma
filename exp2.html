Results for Protein Rich Diet

Dairy Products
Include paneer, milk, and curd which provide good quality protein and calcium. They are easy to include in daily meals and support bone and muscle health.
Pulses & Legumes
Rich in protein and fiber, they are a staple in Indian diets like dal, rajma, and chana. They help in muscle repair and keep you full for a longer time.
Whole Grains
Contain moderate protein along with carbohydrates for sustained energy. Work best when combined with pulses to improve overall protein quality.
