💡 Nutrition Quick Tips
🥗

Eat the Rainbow

Include vegetables of at least 3–4 different colours in your daily meals to ensure a wide range of vitamins and antioxidants.

💧

Hydration Matters

Drink 8–10 glasses of water daily. Herbal teas and coconut water are great alternatives to plain water.

⏰

Meal Timing

Eating at consistent times helps regulate blood sugar. Try not to skip breakfast — it kickstarts your metabolism.

🌾

Whole Grains First

Replace maida roti with whole wheat or multigrain. Brown rice and oats provide more fiber and nutrients than refined grains.

🥜

Healthy Fats

Include a small handful of nuts or a teaspoon of ghee daily. Healthy fats are essential for brain function and vitamin absorption.

🫘

Dal Every Day

Pulses like moong, masoor and chana are the cornerstone of Indian nutrition — rich in protein, iron and fiber. Have at least one serving daily.

📌 Meal Planning Essentials
🍳

Breakfast is Non-Negotiable

A good breakfast with protein and fiber — chilla, oats, or eggs — sets the tone for controlled appetite throughout the day.

🥙

Balanced Lunch Plate

Aim for half the plate as vegetables, a quarter protein (dal/chicken), and a quarter grains (roti/rice). This ratio works universally.

🌙

Light Dinners Work Best

Keep dinners light and protein-focused. Avoid heavy carbs after 7 PM for better sleep and digestion.

🍎

Smart Snacking

Roasted chana, a fruit, or a small handful of nuts between meals keeps your metabolism active and prevents overeating at main meals.

🧬 Understand Your Macros
💪
Protein

Proteins are the building blocks of muscle and tissue. Aim for 0.8–1.2g per kg of body weight. Best Indian sources: dal, paneer, eggs, chicken, rajma, chole, Greek dahi.

⚡
Carbohydrates

Carbs are your body's primary energy fuel. Prefer complex carbs — whole wheat roti, brown rice, oats, daliya — over refined options like maida. They release energy slowly and keep you fuller.

🥑
Healthy Fats

Fats are essential for brain health, hormone production, and vitamin absorption. Include ghee, nuts, seeds, and coconut in moderation. Avoid trans fats in fried packaged foods.

🌿
Fiber

Dietary fiber aids digestion, lowers cholesterol, and keeps blood sugar stable. Target 25–35g daily through vegetables, fruits, legumes, and whole grains. Isabgol is a great supplement.

🍽 Common Indian Meals — Calorie Reference
🫓

Dal Roti (1 serving)

~350–400 kcal · Protein: 14g · Carbs: 58g · Fats: 7g. A staple balanced meal providing slow-release energy and plant protein.

🍚

Rajma Chawal (1 plate)

~520 kcal · Protein: 20g · Carbs: 82g · Fats: 6g. One of the best plant-based complete protein meals in Indian cuisine.

🧀

Paneer Sabzi + 2 Rotis

~480 kcal · Protein: 22g · Carbs: 42g · Fats: 16g. High protein, moderate carb dinner option — ideal post-workout.

🥣

Oats Porridge (1 bowl)

~220 kcal · Protein: 7g · Carbs: 36g · Fats: 4g. Low-calorie, high-fiber breakfast that keeps you full until lunch.

🥚

2-Egg Omelette

~180 kcal · Protein: 13g · Carbs: 2g · Fats: 12g. Quick, high-protein breakfast with near-zero carbs — great for weight loss.

🫘

Moong Dal Chilla (2 pcs)

~260 kcal · Protein: 13g · Carbs: 30g · Fats: 6g. Healthy, light meal with good protein — perfect for breakfast or evening snack.
